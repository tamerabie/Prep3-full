Unit 2: Digital Habits - من صفحة 60-61 Superior

Modals - must/mustn't/can/should:

  • You can't use eraser / pencil - can't = not allowed
  • You mustn't come late - mustn't = prohibition
  • You shouldn't make noise - advice negative
  • You must read privacy policy - necessity (must to → must)
  • You should not be late - advice
  • You can change settings - ability (can to → can)
  • You must take break - advice strong

Conjunctions & Linkers:

Although he is rich, he isn't happy.
Even though it was raining, we went out.
While Ali likes football, Ahmed prefers basketball.

On one hand / On the other hand:
On one hand, studying online is easy. On the other hand, it can be hard to focus at home.
On one hand, cars are fast. On the other hand, they are expensive and pollute.
